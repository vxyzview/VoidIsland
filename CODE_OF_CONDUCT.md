# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want VoidIsland to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone, whatever their age, background, identity, experience or phone. Be kind, assume good intent, and help each other.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people new to Android or to open source.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Accept feedback the same way.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic.
- Never share anyone's private information, including the contents of their notifications.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Sexualised language or attention.
- Spam, trolling or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

The maintainer ([@vxyzview](https://github.com/vxyzview)) may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and may block people who break this code of conduct.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on the [@vxyzview GitHub profile](https://github.com/vxyzview), or use a [private security advisory](https://github.com/vxyzview/VoidIsland/security/advisories/new) if nothing else works. Reports are handled in confidence.
